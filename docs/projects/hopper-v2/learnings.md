## 2026-10-08 (late): Design rule: sections stay on the conveyors' 12 in bolt grid

- **The user's design rule:** most panels are whole feet long, so they match the 12 in bolt patterns of the Kit Conveyor and Light Duty Conveyor.
  - The drop zone is whole typed feet plus `K1XLengthModificationByTypeOfConveyor` (Light Duty −1.6875 in, Kit −1 in, Picking +0.25 in), and the first column K1X absorbs that adjustment.
  - Every section start after it (transition, double transition, K70 pattern of 48 in, K80 filler, elbow) then sits on the 12 in grid. In model inches, start − adjustment is a multiple of 12.
- **Any rule that shortens or lengthens a section must keep whole feet.** The first drop-zone stop (exact, 48.138 in on rev 7) broke this: the transition sat at 49.8 in and the mid panels at 97.8 in, and K80 became 1.85 ft.
- **Fixed:** the stop is now rounded *down* to whole typed feet. Rev 7 gives 4 ft (46.3 in), the side ends at 4.66 in (still ≥ 4), the transition is at 48 in, the mid panels at 96 in, and K80 is 2 ft. Rev 6 gives the same positions, with the side ending at 7.14 in. The `hopper-v2-topcut-limit` check now asserts the grid.
- **Edge case:** if the cut reaches 4 in within the first foot (a very steep cut), the stop rounds to 0 ft, and the drop zone is just the conveyor adjustment, as when 0 is typed today.

## 2026-10-08 (evening): The drop zone now stops where the top cut reaches the minimum panel height

- **User decision:** stop at `PanelMinHeight`. `DZLengthRight` (and `DZLengthLeft` when the left is typed separately) is the typed length, capped at the point where the cut line comes down to `PanelMinHeight`. `RoundDown` keeps that edge at 4 in or more. Every reader (columns, transition, mid section, K80, the model rules) goes through these two variables.
- **Applied to the dev project** (ledger 2026-10-08 16:35). Results:
  - rev 7: 70.3 → 48.1 in, side ends at 4.0 in, the transition follows, 2 × K70, K80 1.85 ft;
  - rev 6: 826 → 55.0 in;
  - no cut, or a cut that stays above 4 in: unchanged;
  - left typed separately: stops on its own cut.
- **Rev 7's hidden left Drop Zone Length still holds 69** (from rev 6). It's harmless while Same Left Side is on.
- Tracked as `hopper-v2-topcut-limit` (fixed-in-dev). Its check fails on the previous version (−1.6 and −4.1 in at the drop-zone end) and passes now.
- **Note:** the drop zone can now come out shorter than typed, with no message (as for short rows).

## 2026-10-08 (evening): Top cut without a limit; bolt zones are not about which wall is taller

- **The user doesn't want a limit on the top-cut angle.** The minimum panel height now drops panels thinner than 4 in at their tail (fixed in dev), but that doesn't close the gap before the transition.
- **Capping the drop zone length where the cut crosses a height works, with no circular reference.** The cap is `Min(input length, (K13OriginZDiff + (DZHeight − height) / TanD(cut)) / 12)`, tested on rev 6 and rev 7.

  | Cap height | Rev 7 drop zone | K20 front edge | Meeting the 12 in transition |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | none (today) | 70.3 in | 0 in | 11.2 in gap |
  | 0 | 59.1 in | 0 in (still a triangle) | side ends at 0, transition 12 in tall |
  | `PanelMinHeight` (4 in) | 48.1 in | 4 in | 4 → 12 in step |
  | transition height (12 in) | 26.2 in | (no K20) | flush |

  The mid section grows to fill Before Elbow Length in every case. Which height to use is the user's choice.
- **`InX0SidePanel` fails whatever the heights.** Zone 3C runs up to the taller wall (`RightBoltZoneTopHeight` = the larger of side and back), which is intended. The other columns give the right shares. `InX0SidePanel` alone returns the zone's end height: 39.5 with the back taller, 40 with the side taller (40 vs 24), and 24 with both at 24.
- **Issues are now tracked per project** in [issues.md](issues.md), generated from `tracking/items.json`.

## 2026-10-08: Production spec 46203 (ARD1652 rev 6, ARD1653 rev 7)

The spec was checked offline from its spec-folder copies, with the method in [../../learnings.md](../../learnings.md) (2026-10-08).
- **Inputs:** AMP Robotics / Victory Blvd, Light Duty Conveyor, 0.125 in, Sparta Gray, offsets 16 in at 45° with a 2.5 in bend, 24 in sides, a 39.5 in back, and a 20° top cut.
- **Rev 7 changes:** Drop Zone Length 69 → 6 ft; back angle on (60°, Backward, ending at 16 in).

- **The 69 was meant as inches.** Drop Zone Length is in feet, and nothing limits it.
  - 69 ft is far beyond the 4 panel columns (about 16 ft), and longer than the 18 ft Before Elbow Length.
  - V2 then silently built no transition and no mid section.
  - Rev 7 (6 ft) built the transition (shape B) and two K70 mid copies, as predicted.
- **The top cut isn't limited, and it can run the side down to 0.**
  - A 20° cut on a 24 in side reaches 0 at 59.1 in, but the rev 7 drop zone ends at 70.3 in. That left an 11.2 in gap with no side wall before the 12 in transition, which is visible in SOLIDWORKS.
  - The same cut made the K20 panels triangles with a zero-height head edge, and K11 a triangle. Those panels had red X's in both revisions.
  - To stay at or above the next section's height, the cut can be at most about 8.8° on rev 7, and 0.8° on rev 6.
- **Rev 6 kept 7 dummies whose rule evaluated to `"Delete"`.** Left 5, 7, 8, 10, 11 (K21, K30, K31, K40, K41) and right <8>, <11> stayed; the right K11 panel was missing altogether.
  - The left survivors are exactly the panels with a negative computed height (top below their origin).
  - But rev 7 also had a negative-height panel (K21), and it was deleted normally.
  - So the trigger is still unknown; it showed only with the 69 ft input. Re-running rev 6 in the sandbox and reading the model generation report would settle it.
- **On the right side, replaced panels take the dummy's number + 1** (K10<2>, K11<3>, K20<5>), in both revisions. The left and back keep the dummy's number. It's consistent, so it most likely comes from how the master numbers the right-side dummies, and it looks harmless.
- **Back top panels get 83° with Back Angle off.**
  - `DZBackAngleDirection` stays "Backward" while the back-angle boxes are hidden, so `DZBackAngleDirectionBackward` is TRUE.
  - The second branch of `PanelListInput`'s BackAngle row then gives 90 − `ConveyorAngle`, and the hidden Conveyor Angle still holds 7 (its DefaultValue is 0, but the saved value 7 carried into the spec).
  - V2 places those panels with no lean (the back origins are gated on Back Angle), so the shape and the placement disagree.
  - **Proposed fix:** gate `DZBackAngleDirectionBackward` on `DZBackAngleOnOff`. Replayed on both revisions: only K21/K31 BackAngle change on rev 6 (83 → 0), and nothing changes on rev 7.
- **`BoltZoneTable` column `InX0SidePanel` returns the zone's end height** (`[5L] - 0`) when the zone starts above row 1, instead of FALSE. So every side K10 gets a zone C of 39.5 in (the back's height) on an 18.5 in panel. K10 built fine with it on rev 6, so it isn't an error on its own.
- **Side bolt zones exist only for K10, K11 and K12;** back panels get them in every column (`Mod(kit,10)`). This is probably intended, since only the first column meets the back wall (inferred).
- **V2 has no `Error` validation on any control.** Kit Conveyor, Light Duty, HandRails, Platforms and Stairs have 1–5 each. V2 already uses rule-driven Minimum/Maximum on Drop Zone Height.
- **Limits proposed** (tested on both revisions with `-OverrideRule`, not yet applied):
  - Drop Zone Length max = `(K1XMaxLengthRight + 3 × PanelMaxLength) / 12`: 16 ft, or 15 when the back leans backward.
  - Before Elbow Length min = Drop Zone Length.
  - Top cut max = `RoundDown(Degrees(ATan((DZHeight − next section height) / (DZLength × 12 − K13OriginZDiff))), 1)`.

  Rev 6 would have been stopped by all three, and rev 7 by the cut. Platform rows show text-box Min/Max may not be enforced (see [../../learnings.md](../../learnings.md), 2026-10-01), so pair each limit with an `Error` message.
- **`BeforeElbowLastPanelLengthRight` came out as −1.8e-15 on rev 7.** Titan treats it as 0, so K80 was correctly deleted.
- **Red X pattern** (details in [../hopper-v2-panels/learnings.md](../hopper-v2-panels/learnings.md)):
  - the back bottom row fails in both revisions;
  - side K11 and K20 fail in both;
  - side K10 fails only with the back angle on (family x531);
  - A31-K50 (left transition) has a warning that the right side doesn't.
- **Applies elsewhere:**
  - Hidden inputs keep their values and still drive rules (Backward, Conveyor Angle).
  - A long input with no maximum can silently remove whole sections.

## 2026-10-02: Read for the layout-app table

*Split from the joint Hopper V2 / Panels entry; the Panels half is in [../hopper-v2-panels/learnings.md](../hopper-v2-panels/learnings.md).*

- **V2 hosts Panels for the drop zone only.** Macro `ReleaseAllPanels` makes one Panels spec per enabled drop-zone panel, at most 33: right K10–K42 (A32), left (A31), back K10–K32 (A30). It loads them through `SinglePannelHostControl` with InputValues `DWCalcPanelListInput` (64 rows), then swaps V2's dummy instances for the panel files. The transition, mid and elbow kits are parts in V2's own model, and Panels sends nothing back.
- **Frame widths cut off controls here too.** V2's CustomerInfo frame is `LeftSideWindowWidth` = 600 px with scroll bars hidden, and Equipment Name, Sticker Name and Safety Parts Color sit at Left 748.
- **The spec ID isn't formatted the same way on both sides.** V2's folder is `<prefix>-Hopper ` & `Text(DWSpecificationID,"0000")`, but it sends Panels `HostedSpecificationId` = the raw `DWSpecificationId`, and Panels builds its folder from that unformatted value. For spec IDs below 1000 the two folder names would differ. This needs a check on a real release.
- **Sandbox registration:** V2 is visible and `Deployed=False`; Panels and the original DW Hopper Project are hidden and `Deployed=False`. DW Order Project doesn't open any hopper.
- **Elbow Angle means different things in the two hoppers.** In the original it is the angle between the runs (180 = straight); in V2 it is the bend itself.
- The full table is in [../inputs/hopper-v2-inputs.md](../inputs/hopper-v2-inputs.md).
